What we heard

The brief

A diagram editor for people writing LaTeX: students, lecturers and researchers who come from a .tex file and go back to one. It should feel exact and crafted, modern and a little playful, not like a SaaS dashboard.

exactcraftedmodernplayful

Success: recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour, says 'diagram' and 'TeX' without a compass, a pencil or Overleaf green.

Concept A

Node to Node

A round node and a square node joined by a backslash, the character every TeX command starts with.

  • Two node shapes, one edge: the smallest possible diagram
  • The edge is a \, so the mark is also TeX
  • Coral and blue are the app's red and blue pens, brightened; indigo is its ink
